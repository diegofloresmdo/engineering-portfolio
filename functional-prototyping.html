CASE 02 / Siemens Healthineers · Internship

From CAD to working demonstrators.

Led project coordination and technical development of a positioning proof of concept, delivering two motorized demonstrators.

Functional prototypes
My roleMechatronics Engineering Intern
PeriodMar — Aug 2026
DisciplinesMechanical design · Embedded

The challenge

Turn an early medical-device positioning idea into a functional proof of concept that engineers and stakeholders could assess.

My contribution

  • Served as the main project coordinator, organizing development activities, progress reviews and deliverables with the engineering team.
  • Contributed to team ideation, concept research and evaluation of design alternatives.
  • Designed mechanical parts, assemblies and interfaces in Siemens NX.
  • Prepared and manufactured 3D-printed components, assembled functional prototypes and refined their mechanical behavior through iterative testing.
  • Developed Arduino-based prototype motion control and supported functional evaluation with the team.
  • Created technical presentations, CAD documentation, test records and handover materials.
  • Presented progress and demonstrated prototype functionality to stakeholders to explain feasibility, limitations and development options.

From idea to stakeholder review

My work connected planning, mechanical design, fabrication, control and communication. Working prototypes made design tradeoffs tangible and supported discussions about the next development steps.

Outcome & scope

Delivered functional demonstrators and supporting technical documentation for continued development. This was exploratory engineering and proof-of-concept work, without a claim of clinical validation or a released medical product.

Tools & methods

Siemens NXTeamcenter3D printingArduinoServo control

Work products

  • Two motorized demonstrators
  • CAD and control code
  • Assembly / testing documentation
  • Stakeholder demonstrations
