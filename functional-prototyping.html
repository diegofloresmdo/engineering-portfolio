CASE 02 / Siemens Healthineers · Internship

From CAD to working demonstrators.

Led project coordination and technical development of a positioning proof of concept, delivering two motorized demonstrators.

Functional prototypes
My roleMechatronics Engineering Intern
PeriodMar — Aug 2026
DisciplinesMechanical design · Embedded

The challenge

Explore mechanical positioning concepts and communicate their feasibility through functional prototypes and stakeholder reviews.

My contribution

  • Conducted concept research and evaluation; coordinated development activities and deliverables with engineers and stakeholders.
  • Designed mechanical assemblies, interfaces and positioning components in Siemens NX.
  • Produced 3D-printed parts, assembled demonstrators and refined designs through testing.
  • Programmed Arduino-based motion control and collaborated on functional tests.
  • Prepared progress presentations, prototype demonstrations and handover documentation.

Engineering focus

Use functional demonstrations to make design tradeoffs tangible during concept reviews.

Outcome & scope

Delivered two motorized demonstrators with CAD data, control code, presentations, testing records and technical documentation. These were proof-of-concept prototypes.
