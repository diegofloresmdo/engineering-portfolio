CASE 01 / Siemens Healthineers · Bachelor thesis

Connecting control to inspection.

Developing communication and coordinated control for automated patient-table inspections.

In progress
My roleBachelor Thesis Student — Automation Engineering
PeriodSep 2026 — Present
DisciplinesAutomation · Robotics

The challenge

Support inspection sequences by connecting table positioning and status feedback with the main PLC and robotic inspection equipment.

My contribution

  • Analyze OPC UA nodes and test control/status signals with UAExpert.
  • Develop PLC sequences and PLC–robot handshakes for readiness, program loading and execution.
  • Develop HMI screens for operation and inspection feedback.
  • Work with the team on robot coordinate frames and integration of sensing and tooling.

Engineering focus

Make command and status behavior explicit before coordinating the next inspection step.

Outcome & scope

Integration and testing are ongoing. This case describes current work, rather than a completed production deployment.
